About A&B Tours and Travels

A family-run travel company from Dindigul, taking travellers to the hill stations of Tamil Nadu and Kerala for over a decade.

Our Story

The Hills Are Our Home Ground

A&B Tours and Travels started in Dindigul with a single cab and a simple idea — that a hill station trip shouldn't mean juggling three different bookings and worrying about hidden costs.

Today we handle everything in one package: the vehicle, the room, the food and the complete day-by-day sightseeing plan. You pay one fixed price, and toll, parking and driver charges are already inside it.

We started with Kodaikanal, two and a half hours from our office, and now run the same trusted trips across the border into Kerala — Munnar's tea estates, Periyar's wildlife at Thekkady, and the green meadows of Vagamon.
